Built for every skin tone

Most lasers can't treat dark skin. This one can.

Older IPL and alexandrite machines struggle on deeper skin tones, because the light can't distinguish pigment in the hair from pigment in the skin. The Alpha Pro solves that with an 808 nm diode wavelength, a long adjustable pulse and a sapphire contact-cooling tip that chills the surface while the follicle heats underneath.

The result: Marzi can safely treat Fitzpatrick I through VI, including brown and Black skin, and tanned Okanagan summer skin that other clinics turn away. Every new client gets a patch test first.

Straight answers

Common questions

Single sessions at Marzi Skincare & Laser Clinic start at $80 for the upper lip and $225 for a full face. Underarms are $95, Brazilian $180 and full legs $290. Packages of three are discounted, and first-time laser clients receive 50% off their first session.

Across Kelowna, expect roughly $70–$120 per session for small areas and $250–$400 for large ones. Judge cost by the full course rather than the session — a cheap session on an underpowered machine usually means more sessions overall. See the full price list.

Most people need 6 to 10 sessions, spaced 4 to 8 weeks apart. Hair grows in cycles and a laser only disables follicles in the active growth phase on the day you are treated, which is why one session can never do it all.

Coarse dark hair on underarms and the bikini line clears fastest. Fine facial hair and hormone-driven growth usually need more sessions plus occasional maintenance.

Yes. The Alpha Pro is an 808 nm diode laser with sapphire contact cooling, which makes it safe across Fitzpatrick I–VI, including brown and Black skin. Deeper tones are treated with lower fluence and longer pulse widths so the follicle heats while surrounding pigment stays protected.

A patch test is performed on every new client before a full session.

Most clients describe a warm snap rather than pain. The cooling tip chills the skin before and after each pulse, and Power Motion Technology moves in continuous passes rather than single high-energy shots, which spreads the sensation out. Underarms and Brazilian are the most sensitive; arms and legs are usually very comfortable. Nearly everyone finds it easier than waxing.

Shave the area 12–24 hours before and arrive with clean skin — no lotion, deodorant, self-tanner or makeup on the treatment area.

Do not wax, pluck, thread or epilate for at least two weeks beforehand: the laser needs an intact root to target. Avoid sun and tanning beds for two weeks, and mention any medication that increases light sensitivity.
